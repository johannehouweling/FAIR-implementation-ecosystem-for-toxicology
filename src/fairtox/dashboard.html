    About the data

    What is listed

    FAIR-enabling resources: standards, databases, policies and guidance, collections, and tools or services that make data or metadata findable, accessible, interoperable or reusable. Projects, initiatives and communities are not listed themselves, only the resources they made.

    Where the resources come from

    The FAIRsharing Toxicology collection and curation by the ELIXIR Toxicology Community. Types follow the FAIRsharing registries; software and services, which FAIRsharing does not register, are listed as tools.

    Lanes

    First published

    When a resource first became public: the online publication of the paper that first describes it, its first release, or the adoption of a guidance or policy. Each date links to its source. Resources without a known date are in the list but not in the figure.

    Versions

    The data are versioned and citable: each release has a version number, and every change is recorded in the repository.
