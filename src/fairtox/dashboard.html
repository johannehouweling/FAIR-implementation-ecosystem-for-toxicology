    About the data

    What is listed

    Standards, databases, policies and guidance, collections, and tools or services that make data or metadata findable, accessible, interoperable or reusable, each typed as a FAIR-Enabling Resource or another FAIR Supporting Resource. Projects, initiatives and communities are not listed themselves, only the resources they made, except communities with a registered FAIR Implementation Community nanopublication: these are in the list but not in the figure.

    Where the resources come from

    The FAIRsharing Toxicology collection and curation by the ELIXIR Toxicology Community. Types follow the FAIRsharing registries; software and services, which FAIRsharing does not register, are listed as tools. FAIRsharing metadata and the declarations of the toxicology FAIR Implementation Profiles (FIPs) are read when the page is built; this version read FAIRsharing on and the FIPs on .

    FSR types and FIP declarations

    Resources are typed in the FIP ontology (), after the typology of FAIR Supporting Resources (FSRs) of Magagna et al. 2024, CEUR-WS Vol-3882: FAIR-Enabling Resource or other FAIR Supporting Resource, one type per resource. A FIP declares which resources its community uses for each FAIR principle. The FIP declarations switch draws dotted lines from each FIP to the resources it declares; the details of a resource list its declarations, with links to their nanopublications.

    Lanes

    Generic FAIR and life-science resources have a lane each. The toxicology lanes, together the toxicology infrastructure, arrange toxicology resources by the data life-cycle phase they act on, the earliest if several (the span lists them all), or, with Toxicology lanes set to Creator, by who made them.

    First published

    When a resource first became public: the online publication of the paper that first describes it, its first release, or the adoption of a guidance or policy. Each date links to its source. Resources without a known date are in the list but not in the figure.

    Versions

    The data are versioned and citable: each release has a version number, and every change is recorded in the repository.

    Data dictionary

    The fields of the dataset (fair-tox-ecosystem.jsonld), with the allowed values of the controlled ones. resources.csv has the same fields, with aliases for alternateName and date for datePublished, and adds key (a stable identifier), label and abbr (as shown in the figure), year, lane and creatorLane (the lane by phase and by creator), registry (the FAIRsharing registry of the type), fairsharing (the FAIRsharing record) and fromCollection (defined below).
